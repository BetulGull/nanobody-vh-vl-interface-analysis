## Key Methods facts extracted (source-verified, not from memory)

- **Zhang regional charge**: net charge at pH 7.4 = sum of D(-1), E(-1), R(+1), K(+1), H(+0.1) over region residues. **Exact match to this project's planned "sequence charge proxy at pH 7.4."**
- **Zhang regional hydropathy**: mean Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy index over region residues (their citation 30 = Kyte & Doolittle 1982, same scale used throughout this project since Stage 4).
- **Zhang FR2_CDR3_Dis**: "minimum distance between the amino acid at position 42 in FR2 and any amino acid in CDR3, excluding the first and last two residues" — identical to the frozen Stage-6 implementation (re-confirmed, not re-derived).
- **Zhang bent/extended classification**: derived from a density-plot trough in THEIR cohort — explicitly not assumed to transfer to our cohort; our own FR2_CDR3_Dis distribution will be inspected independently (Figure 4 Panel A) before any such label is used.
- **Gordon paratope-fraction logic**: CDR-fraction-of-paratope and framework-fraction-of-paratope are directly analogous to our already-frozen Stage-9 `cdr3_contact_fraction`/`framework_contact_fraction`.
- **Raybould PPC/PNC formula**: identical mathematical form to PSH with `H(R,S)` replaced by `|Q(R)|` (absolute charge) — already implemented exactly this way in Stage 8.
- **Raybould SFvCSP**: `sum(Q(RH)) * sum(Q(RL))` over surface-exposed VH/VL residues — genuinely a paired-Fv-only descriptor; **remains NA for VHH per the standing project rule (Stage 8), not computed here.**
