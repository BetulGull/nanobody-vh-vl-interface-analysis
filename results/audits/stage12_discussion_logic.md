# Stage 12 — Discussion Logic (per mechanistic module)

## MODULE 1 — Loss of VL creates a surface problem

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** A reproducible, empirically-derived 28-position former VH-VL interface (Stage 3), validated against a translated-far-away geometric control, spanning FR2, FR3, CDR3, and FR4 — substantially broader than the classical 4-position tetrad (IMGT 42/49/50/52).

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** VL absence exposes a defined, non-trivial surface that a single-domain antibody must structurally accommodate in some way.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** That this surface is problematic in any energetic or thermodynamic sense — no stability or folding-energy measurement was made.

## MODULE 2 — Sequence-level chemical adaptation

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** VHH former-interface intrinsic hydrophobicity (H_intrinsic) differs significantly from conventional VH (standardized effect -0.91, q=4.4e-86).

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** VHH sequences carry systematically different amino-acid chemistry at exactly the positions that would otherwise face VL.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** That this sequence difference alone is sufficient for viability, or that it evolved specifically for this purpose (no evolutionary/selection analysis was performed).

## MODULE 3 — Folded surface realization

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** Native exposed surface chemistry differs VHH vs VH (q=1.8e-253); intrinsic sequence chemistry strongly predicts native surface chemistry (R²=0.52); shielding adds further independent explanatory power (R²→0.58).

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** The sequence-level adaptation is not merely a compositional artifact — it is realized in the actual folded, solvent-exposed structure, and CDR3 shielding provides additional structural modulation.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** Causality in either direction (sequence chemistry does not "cause" the surface state to develop — both are properties of the same folded structure observed simultaneously).

## MODULE 4 — CDR3 geometric accommodation

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** CDR3 removal (geometric SASA counterfactual) increases exposure at the 15-position shieldable interface in every one of 533 VHH structures; CDR3 length/rho/FR2_CDR3_Dis jointly and independently associate with shielding magnitude (R²=0.82); shielding and CDR3-target packing (BSA) are so strongly correlated (r=0.97, VIF=15.5) that they cannot be statistically separated.

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** CDR3 geometry (not shielding and packing as two distinct mechanisms) forms one coupled structural-accommodation module.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** That shielding and packing are mechanistically identical processes at the physical level — only that this specific structural dataset cannot statistically distinguish their independent contributions.

## MODULE 5 — Surface patch / electrostatic phenotype

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** VHH shows significantly lower former-interface PSH and a smaller largest hydrophobic patch than conventional VH; VHH shows altered electrostatic potential magnitude in BOTH positive and negative components (not a uniform shift); both H_intrinsic and shielding independently associate with PSH (R²=0.115→0.230).

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** The reduced/reorganized hydrophobic patch phenotype reflects both sequence-level and CDR3-geometric contributions acting together, not either alone.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** Anything about aggregation propensity, colloidal stability, or developability — PSH/patch descriptors are empirical spatial quantities, not validated aggregation predictors in this project (Stage 10 was not run).

## MODULE 6 — Antigen-interface reorganization

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** VHH shows significantly greater framework antigen-contact participation, greater CDR3 dominance, and greater 15-position former-interface engagement than conventional VH; the former interface remains antigen-accessible in a majority (66.4%) but not all VHH structures.

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** VHH antigen-binding architecture is reorganized — spread across framework, CDR3, and a retained fraction of the former interface — rather than simply lost or universally sealed.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** That this reorganization confers any functional advantage, higher affinity, or broader specificity — contact participation is not binding affinity.

## MODULE 7 — Formal statistical integration

**WHAT WE OBSERVED:** The unadjusted Stage-9 descriptive relationships between shielding and antigen-interface organization (engagement, framework fraction, CDR3 fraction) are robust and highly significant on their own; after formally adjusting for CDR3 geometry (length + FR2_CDR3_Dis), shielding's independent contribution to all three outcomes becomes statistically indistinguishable from zero (p=0.065-0.243), while CDR3 length remains independently significant throughout.

**WHAT IT SUGGESTS:** CDR3 geometry, not CDR3 shielding specifically, is the more fundamental organizing variable connecting structural accommodation to antigen-interface reorganization in this dataset.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE:** That shielding plays no biological role — only that its statistically independent contribution, once CDR3 geometry is accounted for, cannot be reliably separated from that geometry in this dataset. This is the central corrective finding that must be carried into any final narrative.
