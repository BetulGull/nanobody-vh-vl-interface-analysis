## CDR3-relative-position convention — unresolved, not guessed

Zhang's Methods and Results text, as extracted, does **not** describe a normalized/relative CDR3-position coordinate for the length-varying interaction heatmaps (their Fig 4B-D) or the CDR3 amino-acid-usage heatmap (their Fig 3F) — all positional references in the extractable text use raw IMGT numbering. Per the task's explicit instruction ("if an exact definition cannot be recovered, do NOT guess — mark it unresolved"), this axis convention is **independently designed** for Figure 4B, not attributed to Zhang: relative CDR3 position = residue rank within the observed CDR3 (1-indexed from the N-terminal real residue), documented explicitly as a project-designed adaptation, not a reproduction of an unrecoverable Zhang convention.
