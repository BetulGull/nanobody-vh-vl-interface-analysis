Figure 11. 3D localization of former-interface hotspots.

Three deterministically-selected, representative real experimental VHH structures (top; blue cartoon) and three representative conventional VH structures (bottom; purple cartoon, VL shown as translucent pink structural context) from the SAbDab-derived cohort. Exemplars were chosen, independently per group, as the structures nearest to that group's own multivariate centroid in a standardized 7-variable feature space built entirely from existing validated structure-level quantities common to both groups (former-interface PSH/PPC/PNC, 28-position engagement fraction, 28-position mean charge and hydropathy, CDR3 length; candidate pool restricted to each group's own frozen primary nonredundant representative structures with >=90% former-interface coverage) -- not an averaged or artificial structure, and not manually selected. All six structures are Kabsch-aligned onto a single common framework reference (RMSD 0.29-1.40 A over 90 shared IMGT framework Calpha atoms), the same alignment machinery used throughout this project.

On each structure, the empirical former-interface (25/28-position set) is shown as a restrained, semi-transparent surface patch. Gold spheres mark the 14 IMGT positions identified as important for at least one validated mechanistic feature in Figure 10 (sequence divergence, hydropathy shift, charge shift, native exposure difference, CDR3 shielding, antigen engagement); a magenta ring additionally marks the canonical FR2 tetrad (IMGT 42/49/50/52).

Directly beneath each 3D structure is that exact exemplar's own local sequence, shown only for the IMGT positions relevant to the highlighted hotspots (each hotspot plus up to 2 immediate true-sequence neighbors on each side, merged where overlapping) and grouped into separate FR2 / FR3 / CDR3 / FR4 blocks with a visible break wherever the true sequence is not contiguous -- distant positions are never drawn as if adjacent. This is the exemplar's actual sequence, not a cohort consensus or logo; conventional-VH sequence strips show the VH chain only (VL sequence is not included, since the empirical former-interface is a VH-side positional definition). Highlight colors in the sequence strip are identical to the 3D marker colors at the same IMGT position -- gold fill for an important hotspot, magenta border for the FR2 tetrad, no highlight for context-only neighbors -- verified with zero mismatches across all 264 position-by-structure entries (Figure11_exemplar_sequence_mapping.csv).

Full per-position feature membership (which of the six Figure 10 mechanistic features each hotspot belongs to) and exact quantitative values are given in Figure 10 and its source data, not repeated here as on-figure text.

No new interface, SASA, shielding, antigen-contact, APBS, or patch calculation was performed for this figure; exemplar selection is a deterministic visualization procedure over already-validated quantities, not a new biological metric.
