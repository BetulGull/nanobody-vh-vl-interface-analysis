Figure 9. Sequence remodeling at the former VH-VL interface is spatially associated with a distinct 3D surface phenotype in VHH.

This figure traces one evidence chain across four layers, all restricted to the empirical former VH-VL interface (28 IMGT positions; frozen Stage-3 definition; VHH N=533, conventional VH N=1429 unless noted): WHERE the sequence differs, in WHICH chemical direction, WHERE those differences sit on the 3D surface, and how that surface's patch and continuum-electrostatic phenotype differs between VHH and conventional VH.

(A/B) Position-resolved sequence logos (VHH top, conventional VH bottom) over the 28-position former interface, with six thin position-annotation tracks below marking, for each of six downstream quantities, the top-5 positions by value (a predeclared, non-manual ranking rule): sequence divergence (JSD), hydropathy shift, charge shift, native surface exposure, VHH CDR3 shielding, and VHH antigen-contact frequency (secondary track; framework-scoped, absent for CDR3-range positions). Tetrad positions (42, 49, 50, 52) are highlighted gold throughout.

(C) Position-aligned signed physicochemical difference matrix: ΔHydropathy and ΔCharge (diverging scales, centered at zero, VHH minus conventional VH; direction labeled explicitly on each colorbar), and JSD and native-exposure difference (sequential/diverging scales as appropriate). Positions without a defined value (CDR3 shielding is undefined for CDR3-range positions themselves) are shown hatched, never zero-filled.

(D) The top-5 hydropathy-shift and charge-shift positions and the tetrad are mapped onto deterministic median-PSH exemplar structures (VHH pdb_00006jb2-A, conventional VH pdb_00004k2u-I-M; reused unmodified from the Figure 5 revision, framework-aligned, RMSD=1.27 Å).

(E) Hydrophobic surface phenotype (PSH): cohort-level former-interface PSH is lower in VHH (median=40.33, mean=41.01) than conventional VH (median=43.04, mean=44.66; Mann-Whitney p=7.2e-08), shown alongside the same median-exemplar hydropathy-surface renders used in Figure 5.

(F/G) Charged-patch phenotype (PPC/PNC): because both scores are zero-inflated, each is reported as two separate tests, never one pooled boxplot. Positive-patch (PPC) prevalence is higher in VHH (48.4%, 258/533) than conventional VH (22.5%, 322/1429; Fisher exact p=1.2e-27, OR=3.23); among patch-positive structures, conditional PPC magnitude is also modestly higher in VHH (median 0.094 vs 0.061; Mann-Whitney p=0.006, rank-biserial r=-0.13). Negative-patch (PNC) prevalence is likewise higher in VHH (41.7%, 222/533) than conventional VH (27.4%, 391/1429; Fisher exact p=2.2e-09, OR=1.90), with a smaller conditional-magnitude difference (median 0.193 vs 0.160; p=0.010, r=-0.12). The paired 3D surfaces show the existing, amino-acid-identity-based charge_class classification (ARG/LYS/HIS blue, ASP/GLU red) restricted to the 28-position former interface -- this is a visualization of an existing per-residue classification, not a validated patch-membership decomposition; per-position PPC/PNC patch-membership data do not exist in the validated project outputs and are not shown or invented anywhere in this figure.

(H) Continuum electrostatic phenotype (APBS): positive and negative potential components are always kept separate (never netted). The positive component is modestly higher in VHH (mean=118.3 vs 115.4 kT/e, N=521/1386, p=3.5e-06); the negative component is more negative in VHH (mean=-168.2 vs -158.0 kT/e, p=5.4e-38). The paired 3D surfaces render the real APBS potential grid directly (not a residue-level decomposition), with identical +/-5 kT/e color limits for both groups (a fixed rendering convention, not a data-derived value). The conventional-VH APBS exemplar (pdb_00003gi9-H-L) required one documented, necessary substitution for this module only, because the Figure-5 VH median exemplar lacks a valid APBS grid (see figure9_sequence_structure_surface_audit.md).

Interpretation: VHH shows coordinated sequence-level remodeling of the former VH-VL interface -- concentrated in FR2 and the tetrad for raw sequence divergence, but extending into FR3 and CDR3 for charge shift and native exposure -- that is spatially associated with, and co-localizes with, a distinct hydrophobic- and charged-patch phenotype and a distinct continuum electrostatic phenotype at the same surface region. This figure does NOT claim these differences cause, predict, or are sufficient for any downstream biophysical or functional outcome. Solubility, aggregation resistance, thermodynamic/conformational stability, and binding affinity were not calculated or tested anywhere in this analysis, and no predictor for any of them was used. The APBS layer in particular is a non-local, whole-surface continuum property; it is described only as spatially corresponding to or co-localizing with the sequence/patch layers above it, never as caused by or attributable to any individual residue.
