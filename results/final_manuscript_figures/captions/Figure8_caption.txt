Figure 8. Feature-specific sequence remodeling at local former-interface positions.

Six independently readable panels, each addressing one validated mechanistic feature at the former VH-VL interface: (A) sequence divergence, (B) hydropathy shift, (C) charge shift, (D) native exposure difference, (E) CDR3 shielding (VHH only), (F) antigen engagement. Important IMGT positions for each feature are the same top-5 list already established and audited in Figure 10 (a predeclared, non-manual ranking, not re-derived here).

Within each panel, VHH (top) and conventional VH (bottom) position-resolved sequence logos are shown side by side at identical IMGT coordinates, restricted to a local sequence window around each important position (up to 2 true-sequence neighbors on each side, overlapping windows merged). Where important positions fall in distant parts of the domain, separate local blocks are shown with a visible gap and a region label (FR2/FR3/CDR3/FR4) -- distant positions are never drawn as if sequentially adjacent. Amino-acid letter colors encode residue identity/class (established Gordon-inspired manuscript palette); a translucent feature-colored band marks each important (hotspot) position, while +/-2 context positions remain unhighlighted. The canonical FR2 tetrad (IMGT 42/49/50/52) is labeled in bold beneath every panel where it appears and additionally receives a thin magenta outline only when it is also an important hotspot for that specific feature -- tetrad membership is a constant structural fact shown independently of per-feature importance.

Sequence logos are heavy-variable-domain-only summaries (VHH, conventional VH); light-chain sequence is never included, since the empirical former-interface is a VH-side positional definition. Underlying amino-acid frequencies are the existing frozen Stage-4 position-level distributions (X-dropped, renormalized), unchanged from Figures 1/2/9. No interface derivation, sequence curation, FreeSASA, Arpeggio, APBS, or antigen-contact calculation was rerun for this figure, and no solubility, aggregation, stability, or affinity value was calculated.
