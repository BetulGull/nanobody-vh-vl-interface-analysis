Figure 4A. CDR3-former-interface geometry, VHH versus conventional VH.

(A) Deterministic structural context: the VHH exemplar (pdb_00008qf4-E) and conventional-VH exemplar (pdb_00009f1i-H-L, VL shown translucent for context only) were each selected as the structure nearest its group's median fraction of the 15-position former interface contacted by CDR3 (distance-from-median = 0 in both cases; both structures resolution <=1.4 A, full interface coverage), common-framework aligned (89 IMGT positions, RMSD=1.11 A). CDR3 (red), the 15-position empirical former-interface target (orange), and the canonical tetrad 42/49/50/52 (magenta spheres, annotation only) are shown; the target is the full empirically derived former-interface surface, not the tetrad or FR2 alone.

(B-D) Cohort-level contact-probability heatmaps: rows are the 15-position SHIELDABLE_NON_CDR3_INTERFACE (Stage-6 definition, tetrad rows in bold gold, FR2 rows in italic); columns are internal CDR3 residues (excluding the first/last two real CDR3 residues, matching the FR2_CDR3_Dis convention), exact IMGT position/insertion-code labels retained where at least 10 evaluable structures exist in either group (22 of 59 raw labels; deeper single-observation insertion positions are excluded from the plot for readability only and retained in full in the source data). Contact = minimum heavy-atom distance <=4.5 A (this project's own Gordon-verified primary contact threshold, reused from Stage 2/9; sensitivity at 4.0/5.0 A in source data, direction and significance unchanged at all three). Cell value = evaluable-structure contact probability (contacts / evaluable pairs), never zero-filled for missing pairs. (B) VHH. (C) Conventional VH. (D) Signed VHH-minus-VH difference, diverging scale centered exactly at zero, symmetric limit = max observed |delta P|; not a significance map.

(E) Global per-structure geometry (Mann-Whitney U, BH-FDR q across the 8-metric family in Figure4A_global_geometry_statistics.csv): fraction of the 15-position interface contacted, minimum CDR3-to-interface distance, fraction of contacts allocated to FR2, and FR2_CDR3_Dis (exact frozen Stage-6 definition, reused unmodified, newly computed for VH using native VH coordinates with VL never loaded).

N: VHH=533, conventional VH=1428-1429 (one VH structure excluded from FR2_CDR3_Dis/global-geometry family: <=4 real CDR3 residues, cannot define an "internal" CDR3 subset).

Key findings: VHH CDR3 contacts a significantly broader fraction of the 15-position interface (mean 0.34 vs 0.27, q=2.3e-35) and sits significantly closer to FR2/IMGT42 (FR2_CDR3_Dis mean 7.95 vs 8.65 A, q=1.2e-54). Contact is not FR2-exclusive in either group: a strong, VHH-enriched hotspot exists at FR3 position 66 (non-tetrad, non-FR2), and conventional VH's contacts are proportionally more FR2-concentrated than VHH's (77% vs 72% of contacts in FR2, q=1.0e-27) even though VHH's absolute FR2 contact burden is higher. These are structural/geometric associations only; no solubility, stability, or functional claim is made.
