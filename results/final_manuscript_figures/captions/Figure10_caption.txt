Figure 10. Local sequence-to-property remodeling at the former-interface.

Six feature-specific modules, each directly connecting local sequence composition to a position-specific quantitative result: (A) sequence divergence, (B) hydropathy shift, (C) charge shift, (D) native exposure, (E) CDR3 shielding (VHH only), (F) antigen engagement. Important IMGT positions for each feature are the same predeclared top-5-by-value ranking already established and audited for Figure 8/Figure 10 (not re-derived here).

Each module reads top to bottom: a VHH cohort sequence logo, a conventional-VH cohort sequence logo (identical IMGT coordinates and y-axis scale in both), and immediately below both logos a compact quantitative strip giving the actual position-level feature value at the same IMGT positions -- so a reader can move vertically from a position's sequence composition directly to its feature value without searching elsewhere in the figure. Local sequence context is restricted to each important position plus up to 2 true-sequence neighbors on each side (overlapping windows merged); where important positions fall in distant parts of the domain, separate local blocks are shown with a visible gap and a region label (FR2/FR3/CDR3/FR4) so that widely separated positions are never drawn as if adjacent. Amino-acid letter colors encode residue identity/class (established Gordon-inspired manuscript palette, unchanged from Figures 1/2/8/9); a translucent feature-colored band marks each important position in both the logo and the strip below it, while +/-2 context positions and their quantitative cells remain pale/neutral. The canonical FR2 tetrad (IMGT 42/49/50/52) is labeled in bold on every IMGT axis where it appears and receives an additional thin magenta outline (on both the logo highlight and the quantitative cell) only when it is also an important hotspot for that specific feature.

Quantitative-strip semantics (verified from source data, not assumed): sequence divergence (JSD) is unsigned -- white-to-teal intensity is divergence magnitude only. Hydropathy shift, charge shift, native exposure, and antigen engagement are signed VHH-minus-conventional-VH differences on diverging scales centered at zero (hydropathy: hydrophilic <-> hydrophobic; charge: negative <-> positive; exposure: VH more exposed <-> VHH more exposed; engagement: VH engages more <-> VHH engages more, using the existing frozen Stage-9 difference column). CDR3 shielding is VHH-only -- a geometric quantity (mean surface area occluded by CDR3 presence) with no conventional-VH equivalent by definition, shown on its own sequential violet scale, explicitly labeled "VHH only". A gray hatched cell marks a position where the metric is structurally undefined (e.g. CDR3 shielding cannot be defined for a position inside CDR3 itself); this is never invented or interpolated. Exact numeric values are printed at every important position.

No 3D structures appear in this figure (see Figure 11 for 3D localization). No residue-level PPC, PNC, PSH, or APBS attribution is shown anywhere, because no validated position-resolved data exist for these patch/continuum metrics. This figure does not claim solubility, aggregation resistance, stability, or binding affinity for any position.
