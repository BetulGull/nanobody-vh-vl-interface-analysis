Figure 6. CDR3-former-interface packing: VHH versus conventional VH.

(A) CDR3 length versus CDR3-former-interface buried surface area (BSA) -- the pairwise mutual buried surface area between CDR3 (IMGT 105-117, all real residues) and the empirically derived, frozen 14-position packing target ({40,42,44,49,50,52,55,66,67,68,69,103,119,120}), computed by FreeSASA (Lee-Richards, 1.4 A probe) as SASA(CDR3 alone) + SASA(target alone) - SASA(CDR3+target together), in native experimental coordinates. VHH N=533; conventional VH N=1429 (VH heavy-chain-only coordinates from the native paired VH-VL structure -- VL omitted from this specific calculation, never remodeled, relaxed, or treated as an experimentally isolated domain). Lines and 95% confidence bands are the group-specific fitted relationships from the single predeclared primary model, BSA ~ CDR3_length + group + CDR3_length:group (HC3-robust standard errors, chosen a priori given the expectation that BSA variance increases with CDR3 length, not chosen post hoc). VHH slope = 33.4 A^2/residue; conventional-VH slope = 6.2 A^2/residue; interaction coefficient (Delta-slope) beta3 = 27.2 A^2/residue (95% CI [24.5, 30.0], p<0.001) -- the length-dependent increase in former-interface packing is significantly steeper in VHH. This conclusion is robust to restricting the comparison to the CDR3-length range where both cohorts have adequate representation (common support [5,26] residues, beta3=26.6, same direction and significance) and survives two independent size-normalization checks that rule out the raw result being a trivial consequence of longer CDR3s simply having more residues: BSA per CDR3 residue (beta3=1.35 A^2/residue, q=7.1e-21) and a bounded packing fraction, CDR3-side burial / CDR3's own isolated surface area (beta3=0.0066, q=1.6e-34) -- both remain significantly steeper in VHH.

(B/C) Deterministically selected VHH structural examples (nearest-to-Q25 and nearest-to-Q75 CDR3 length among coverage-complete representatives, distance from target=0 in both cases) illustrating the intradomain packing geometry at shorter versus longer CDR3 length. VHH body in blue, CDR3 in teal, the former-interface packing target in orange. These two structures illustrate the mechanism visually; they are not themselves the evidence for the group comparison, which rests on the full-cohort model in Panel A.

Interpretation: because the length-dependent packing relationship is significantly steeper in VHH than in conventional VH, and this holds after both common-support restriction and size normalization, this result supports a VHH-specific, length-dependent intradomain structural accommodation pattern -- CDR3 participates in length-dependent packing against the former VH-VL interface to a greater extent in VHH than would be expected from a general VH-fold relationship alone. No claim of solubility, aggregation resistance, thermodynamic stability, binding affinity, or causal compensation is made; this is a structural/geometric association only.

Note on relationship to prior literature (not a reproduction): this analysis is conceptually motivated by Zhang et al. 2025's observation that longer VHH CDR3s show increased intramolecular interactions with CDR1/FR2/CDR2 and shorter FR2_CDR3_Dis, but the primary metric here is buried surface area against the empirically derived former VH-VL interface, not Zhang's residue-contact-count metric, and is conceptually distinct from Zhang's own Figure 6 (VHH-antigen interface size), which addresses a different biological quantity entirely; the closer conceptual precedent is Zhang's Figure 4.
