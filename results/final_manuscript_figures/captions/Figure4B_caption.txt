Figure 4B. CDR3 length-dependent structural organization: a comparative adaptation of Zhang et al. 2025's Figure 4 analytical logic to a VHH-versus-conventional-VH comparison (not a reproduction of Zhang's original species comparison).

(A) Deterministic Q25/Q75 CDR3-length structural exemplars for each group (short/long, never "bent"/"extended"; nearest-to-target-quantile, distance=0 in all four cases, common-framework aligned to the VHH-short reference, 84-86 IMGT positions, RMSD 0.73-0.98 A). CDR1 (gold), FR2 (teal), CDR2 (green), CDR3 (red), IMGT42 (magenta sphere) colored consistently across all four panels; conventional-VH VL shown translucent for context only.

(B-D) Paired VHH/conventional-VH interaction-probability heatmaps for CDR3-CDR1, CDR3-FR2, and CDR3-CDR2, using Zhang's own reported interaction threshold (4 A minimum heavy-atom distance, distinct from this project's own 4.5 A Gordon-verified convention used in Figure 4A), identical residue-pair denominator/threshold/color-scale/axis-ordering in both groups. CDR3 x-axis: exact IMGT position/insertion code retained where at least 10 evaluable structures exist in either group (26 of 59 raw labels; Zhang's own CDR3-relative-position convention could not be recovered from the extractable source text -- this exact-IMGT axis is used instead, not a guessed substitute).

(E) CDR3-length relationships: two-row Pearson-correlation matrix (VHH top, conventional VH bottom; Zhang's own confirmed correlation method, not chosen post-hoc) for eleven reproducible/adapted descriptors -- CDR3-CDR1/FR2/CDR2 interaction count, FR2_CDR3_Dis, domain SASA, surface hydrophobicity (sum of domain SASA x Kyte-Doolittle, Zhang's exact formula, reusing the frozen Stage-5 per-residue FreeSASA table with VL never loaded for the VH group), and CDR3 secondary-structure state fractions (DSSP G/T/E/S/C, Zhang's own narrowed reporting set; native experimental coordinates, no minimization; DSSP's blank/unassigned state is mapped to Zhang's "C"/coil). Thin annotation strip below reports the formal length x group interaction test (Y ~ CDR3_length + group + CDR3_length:group, HC3-robust, group=1 for VHH) direction and BH-FDR significance for the same eleven descriptors, separated by a vertical divider between the interaction/geometry/SASA block and the secondary-structure block. Full beta3 coefficients, 95% CIs, and q-values in Figure4B_panelE_interaction_models.csv, not printed in the matrix cells.

Key results (BH-FDR q<0.05 unless noted): the CDR3-length-dependent increase in CDR3-FR2 interaction count is strongly VHH-specific (slope_VHH=0.55/residue vs slope_VH=0.08/residue, beta3=+0.47, q=6.6e-56) and survives length-normalization (q=2.3e-12); CDR3-CDR2 interactions show the same pattern, weaker (beta3=+0.20, q=3.1e-11); CDR3-CDR1 interactions show NO significant group difference in the raw metric (q=0.50) despite both groups' own within-group correlations being significant -- illustrating why a single formal beta3 test, not two separate within-group p-values, is required to claim a group difference; FR2_CDR3_Dis decreases with CDR3 length more steeply in VHH (beta3=-0.19, q=3.5e-23); domain SASA increases with length in both groups but far less steeply in VHH (beta3=-31.1, q=6.3e-28); CDR3 3-turn-helix (G-state) fraction increases with length specifically in VHH (beta3=+0.0083, q=9.5e-14) while beta-strand (E-state) fraction decreases more steeply in VHH (beta3=-0.0156, q=5.8e-12).

Solubility was not calculated anywhere in this figure, per explicit instruction -- no predictor was run and no project descriptor (PSH, APBS, H_native) was relabeled as a solubility score.

N: VHH=533, conventional VH=1429 (1428-1429 depending on metric; FR2_CDR3_Dis and CDR3-internal-residue metrics exclude 1 VH structure with <=4 real CDR3 residues). Common-support sensitivity (CDR3 length restricted to VHH's observed range, [5,26], N_VH=1425) and CDR3-length-normalized sensitivity (interaction count / CDR3 length, to test whether raw-count differences merely reflect longer CDR3s having more residues) are reported in full in Figure4B_common_support_models.csv and Figure4B_length_normalized_sensitivity.csv; both are discussed explicitly in the accompanying report, including where the normalized result diverges from the raw result.

Zhang reference: Zhang et al. 2025 (see references/Zhang_2025.pdf), Figure 4 and associated Methods -- this figure adapts that analytical logic to a new biological comparison and is not a reproduction of the original species-comparison figure.
