Figure 7. Antigen engagement of the former VH-VL interface: VHH versus conventional VH.

(A) Cohort-level distribution of the 28-position engagement fraction -- the fraction of the empirically derived former VH-VL interface (25 unique IMGT positions; frozen Stage-3 definition) that contacts antigen (minimum heavy-atom distance <=4.5 A, heavy-chain domain only) -- for VHH (N=533, blue) and conventional VH (N=1429, lavender, color blended from the VH-purple/VL-pink structural palette used in Panel C). Median lines, whiskers, and box outlines are dark charcoal throughout (orange is reserved exclusively for antibody-antigen contact residues in Panels B/C, never used for statistical annotation).

(B/C) The same quantity visualized on a validated matched-antigen, overlapping-epitope structural pair (interleukin-4 receptor alpha, IL4Ralpha; validation: 99.4% antigen sequence identity, remapped contact Jaccard=0.92, independently corroborated by antigen structural superposition -- see figure7_matched_epitope_validation.md). Panels B and C are rendered in a common reference frame obtained by Kabsch-superposing the two antigen chains directly (RMSD=2.34 A over 176 common Cα, the same transform validated during epitope matching, not re-derived), so the antigen orientation is directly comparable between panels; only the antibody architecture differs.

Color semantics (identical in B and C): gray = antigen; dark gray = the validated matched-epitope residues on the antigen; yellow = former-interface positions NOT contacting antigen in this specific structure; red = former-interface positions that DO contact antigen (red is a subset of yellow -- together they are the full 25-position former-interface, i.e. the denominator and numerator of Panel A's metric); orange = other antibody residues contacting antigen outside the former-interface set; blue = VHH; purple = conventional VH; pink = VL (structural context only -- the former-interface belongs to the VH side and VL residues are never classified as part of it).

(B) VHH-IL4Ralpha (pdb_00008k4q): 28-position engagement = 0.40 (10/25 former-interface positions contact antigen). (C) Conventional VH-VL-IL4Ralpha (pdb_00006wgl), matched epitope: 28-position engagement = 0.28 (7/25).

Structure-specific residue strips below Panels B and C map the authoritative former-interface IMGT positions to their amino-acid identities and antigen-engagement states in the displayed complexes; red denotes antigen-engaged former-interface residues, yellow denotes evaluable non-engaged former-interface residues, and gray denotes positions not evaluable in the structure. IMGT positions 42, 49, 50, and 52 are outlined to indicate the canonical FR2 tetrad.

Interpretation: VHH shows greater cohort-level use of the former VH-VL interface for antigen engagement than conventional VH (Panel A). The matched IL4Ralpha example (Panels B/C) illustrates how residues belonging to the same empirically defined former-interface become part of the antigen-contacting surface (red) to different extents under the two antibody architectures. Panels B/C are single deterministic structural illustrations of this pattern, not independent evidence of a cohort-wide effect -- the cohort-level statistics in Panel A are the primary evidence. No claim of binding affinity, a universal difference in binding angle or interface size, or any functional/energetic advantage is made from these two structures.
