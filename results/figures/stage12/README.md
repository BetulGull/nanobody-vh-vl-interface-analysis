# Stage 12 Figures

Five final integration figures. See `figure_manifest.csv` for full provenance and `results/audits/stage12_integrated_mechanism.md` for the complete synthesis.

- **stage12_fig_final_mechanism.png** — the final integrated mechanism diagram. Every arrow is drawn from `stage12_mechanism_edges.csv`; no decorative or unsupported connections. Solid = robust/direct evidence, dashed = descriptive-only (not independent after adjustment), double-line = highly coupled/inseparable.
- **stage12_fig_position_level_integration.png** — refined former-interface position map (VL burial vs VHH/VH antigen engagement, tetrad marked).
- **stage12_fig_final_effect_evidence.png** — final adjusted-effect forest plot, including the three attenuated/null shielding coefficients (Model4C/5B/6B) shown in red, not hidden.
- **stage12_fig_vhh_vs_conventional_summary.png** — compact summary of the seven strongest validated VHH-vs-conventional group differences.
- **stage12_fig_raybould_style_vhh_vs_vh_psh_surface.png** (+ `.pdf`/`.tiff`) — Raybould-et-al.-2019-style physicochemical molecular surface figure, adapted from their Fig. 2 (CDR-vicinity target) to this project's frozen 28-position empirical former VH-VL interface. Panel A is a full-cohort (N=533 VHH, N=1429 VH) normalized PSH histogram; Panels B-E are deterministic, predeclared 95th-percentile-PSH illustrative exemplars (one VHH, one VH; selected and frozen in `results/structures/stage12_raybould_style_high_psh_exemplars.csv` before any rendering), real PyMOL molecular surfaces with color = per-residue normalized Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy (never the PSH score itself), white outside the empirical interface. See `results/audits/stage12_raybould_fig2_visualization_source_audit.md` for exactly what is reproduced from the source paper vs this project's own adaptation, and `results/audits/stage12_raybould_style_surface_validation.csv` for the 20-point validation (all pass).
